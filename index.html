Know what you're seeing

Trust starts with context.

We label the limits of our data instead of disguising them. That is part of the product.

01 / SOURCE

Reference, not promise.

Quotes are retrieved from third-party public endpoints when available. Availability, latency, coverage and terms are controlled by those providers.

02 / METHOD

Simple maths, visible assumptions.

Gold per gram is estimated from the troy-ounce spot quote (31.1034768 grams) and selected purity. FX conversion uses a separate reference-rate feed.

03 / RESPONSIBILITY

Information, not advice.

Aurel is an informational project, not a broker, exchange or financial adviser. Data can be delayed or wrong; verify important figures independently.

i

Market information is provided for general information only and is not investment, trading, tax or legal advice. Prices are indicative, may be delayed, and may not reflect executable quotes or local-market premiums. Always confirm data with a trusted provider before making financial decisions.
