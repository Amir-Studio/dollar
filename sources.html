DATA & METHODOLOGY

Every number needs context.

AUREL uses public third-party endpoints for demonstration and reference. Data availability, licensing, latency and accuracy depend on those providers. Values are not guaranteed to be real-time or suitable for execution.

Gold reference

The site requests a spot reference from Gold-API's public endpoint. The displayed price may be unavailable or delayed. Confirm the provider's current terms and permitted use before public commercial deployment.

Foreign exchange

The currency converter requests the USD-base reference feed from open.er-api.com. Rates may be refreshed on the provider's schedule rather than continuously. Any IRR cross-rate calculated from this feed is a mathematical reference—not an Iranian free-market, cash, or exchange-office quote.

Gold calculator

The estimator converts troy ounces to grams using 31.1034768 grams per troy ounce, then multiplies by weight and selected purity. It excludes making charges, dealer spreads, taxes, shipping, local premiums and currency conversion fees.

Chart status

The current line visualization is illustrative only; it is not a historical price series and its period controls do not fetch historical data. A genuine historical chart requires a verified, licensed historical data source and clear timestamps.

Commercial readiness

Before relying on or monetizing this site, verify data-source terms, CORS behavior, uptime, rate limits, regional eligibility and any required attribution. Do not treat these references as financial advice.
