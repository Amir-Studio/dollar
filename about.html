OUR PRINCIPLES

Clarity is a product decision.

AUREL is an independent market-reference project designed to make gold and currency information easier to explore. It aims to distinguish reference data from local prices, estimates from executable quotes, and useful context from financial advice.

01

Be explicit

When data is missing or a feature is not connected, the interface should say so. A decorative chart must never be mistaken for historical market data.

02

Show the assumptions

Calculators disclose their units, conversion basis and exclusions. Local taxes, premiums and fees are not silently guessed.

03

Respect the reader

Readable typography, restrained visual hierarchy, responsive layouts and accessible controls are core quality requirements—not decoration.

What AUREL is not

AUREL is not a broker, exchange, portfolio manager or regulated financial adviser. The site does not promise investment outcomes, and public data feeds can be delayed, incomplete or unavailable.
